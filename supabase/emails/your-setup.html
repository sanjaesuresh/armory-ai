How to use this in Claude

1. Create a new Project
Open claude.ai and click "Projects" in the left sidebar. Then click "Create project".
2. Name your Project
Give your project a memorable name (for example, the name of the role or brand you are configuring). Click "Create" to confirm.
3. Paste the custom instructions
In the Project settings panel, open the "Custom instructions" field. Copy the instruction block below and paste it in, then save.
4. Upload knowledge files
